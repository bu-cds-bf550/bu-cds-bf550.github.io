The synthesis project launches: choose a biological question you care about, design a generative
process for it, and show what a method can and cannot recover from it. It is the same move you will
have made twelve times by then — the difference is that this time you pick the question.

**The Act III exam is Mon Dec 7**, in the first session — closed book, no AI, code reading,
covering units 10–12. The project launches in the rest of that session, and from there every
meeting is project studio, with instructors and TAs in the room.

The project proposal is due **Wed Dec 9** and the project bundle during the finals period;
**there is no exam during finals period.**

Unit 13 carries the term's last meeting, Wed Dec 9. It has two sessions: class on Mon Sep 28 was
cancelled, and on Oct 13 evaluation became two units, and each moved every later meeting one place. The last graded problem set,
**PS10, was due Mon Nov 30**; nothing is due in this unit but the project.
